How to use Product on Shelf

A 60-second ballpark price for any product — no vendor round-trip. New here? Follow the six steps below, then start your first estimate.

For sales & presale
1
Pick a product

From Start estimate or the left sidebar — Hardware (firewall, switch, server/HCI, storage…) or Software (Microsoft 365, Windows Server, backup…).

2
Choose the project

Some products have tabs — Replacement, New install or Refresh. Pick the one that matches the deal.

3
Enter the requirements

Quantities (Units of devices), specs, term, SLA and location. The defaults are sensible — just change what you know.

4
Compare the options

Two or three cards appear with a full cost breakdown. The card ringed in orange is the Best value / Recommended pick.

5
Add to estimate

Click Add to estimate on the card you want. One line per product category — re-adding from that category replaces its line.

6
Review & total

Open Custom estimate. Expand any line (the chevron) to see what’s included; add free-form custom lines for anything without its own flow. The grand total sits at the bottom.

Good to know
  • All prices are a ballpark and already include a 20% buffer, in THB. Live prices will come from the price sheet (coming soon).
  • Best value = the cheapest option that meets the sizing. For plan tiers (e.g. Microsoft 365), Recommended is a sensible default, not the cheapest.
  • Server / HCI auto-sizes the node count from the workload — you can override Units of devices (minimum 3).
  • This is for qualifying a deal fast — not a formal quote.
